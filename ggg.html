01Is Monica legit?
Yes. Monica is operated by Monica Technologies Limited, a Nigerian-registered company aligned with the SEC's Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP) framework and NDPA data protection. Monica serves 500,000+ verified users and has processed over ₦500 billion in transactions.
02How fast does Monica pay out to my bank?
Once your crypto deposit confirms onchain, naira credits to your Monica balance instantly. Bank withdrawal runs over NIBSS instant transfer digital banks like Opay and Kuda clear in 5–10 seconds, tier 1 banks like GTBank and Zenith in 30–60 seconds.
03What does Monica charge to convert crypto to naira?
0% on conversion and 0% on bank withdrawal. The only cost is the onchain network fee for sending your crypto, which goes to blockchain validators, not Monica.
04Which coins and networks can I sell on Monica?
Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Tron and BNB, plus USDT and USDC across all supported chains including Bitcoin mainnet, ERC20, TRC20, BEP20, Solana and Base.
05Which Nigerian banks does Monica pay into?
All NIBSS-connected Nigerian banks, including GTBank, Access, First Bank, Zenith, UBA, Fidelity, Stanbic IBTC, Sterling, Union, Wema, Opay, Kuda, Moniepoint, PalmPay and 40+ more.
06Do I need KYC to use Monica?
Yes one screen. BVN or NIN plus a quick selfie. Verification is required for SEC-aligned compliance and bank withdrawals.
07Can I sell USDT TRC20 for naira on Monica?
Yes. Monica supports USDT on TRC20, ERC20, BEP20 and Solana, all with automatic conversion to naira and no conversion fee.
08Is Monica safer than Binance P2P or Bybit P2P?
Monica works differently from any P2P marketplace: it pays naira from its own settlement account, so you never receive funds from a stranger and you avoid the EFCC account freeze consideration that arises on P2P platforms generally when buyers pay with funds from compromised accounts. Binance itself delisted all naira (NGN) pairs in March 2024, so there has been no NGN market on Binance P2P since.
09What is the best app to sell crypto in Nigeria in 2026?
For Nigerians cashing crypto into naira, Monica is a strong choice on payout speed (under 60 seconds after confirmation), fees (0%), bank coverage (40+) and SEC VASP alignment. Alternatives include Quidax, Busha, Yellow Card and Breet.
10Where can I sell crypto for naira without a P2P marketplace?
Monica is a direct crypto to naira off ramp, not a P2P marketplace. Send your USDT, BTC or other coins to your Monica address and receive naira in your Nigerian bank in seconds. No merchant and no order book required.
11Does Monica have a virtual dollar card?
Yes. Verified Monica users can create and fund a virtual USD card and spend globally anywhere Visa or Mastercard is accepted.
12Is the Monica app free to download?
Yes Monica is free on the App Store and Google Play, with no subscription. There are no conversion or withdrawal fees either; you only ever pay the blockchain network fee on a deposit.
